GA Sambosa

Help the sambosa escape the hot oil. Jump through the gaps in the fire!

How to play

  • Press Space or ↑, or click, to jump.
  • Fly through the gaps in the fire to score a point.
  • Catch the mini sambosas to grow. A big sambosa can take a hit and shrink.
  • Touching the fire as a small sambosa, or falling into the oil, ends the game.
  • Reach the score goal at the top to win!
